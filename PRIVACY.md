# Privacy

The app is a window around [app.vallaury.it](https://app.vallaury.it). What the service does with your data is described in its [privacy policy](https://app.vallaury.it/privacy). This page covers the app itself.

**Network.** The app talks to `app.vallaury.it` and, to look for a new version, to the releases of this repository on GitHub. It talks to nobody else.

**On your computer.** The app keeps the website's cookies and cache in its data folder, and the size and position of its window.

**Notifications.** If you turn them on, the app asks `app.vallaury.it` for news every few minutes, using a key of its own. Nothing goes through the push services of Google, Apple or Microsoft. The key is kept in your system's secret store, or only in memory if the system has none, and is erased when you sign out.

**Permissions.** The site may show notifications and copy to the clipboard. Camera, microphone and location are refused.

**Signing in with Google.** The sign-in opens your system browser. To finish it, the app holds a random secret in memory for ten minutes and gives it only to `app.vallaury.it`.

Questions about your data: <privacy@vallaury.it>.
